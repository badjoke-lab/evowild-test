## Why v1 does not pass

The new appendages are structurally split, but visually too small, thin and needle-like.

Against the authority FOOT STRUCTURE panel:

- SIDE lacks the larger low wedge / hoof-like contact mass;
- FRONT split prongs are too narrow and read as tiny fork tips;
- FRONT34 reads as short sticks appended to the ankle, not a purpose-built racing foot;
- BACK split is too weak at normal review scale;
- the rear stabilizer is not integrated into a coherent foot silhouette.

The authority uses a compact but visibly substantial specialized foot: two dominant split blades with a stronger proximal base/contact wedge, plus only subordinate rear support.
