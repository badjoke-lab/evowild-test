# S Authority R0-A4 v2 Review

Decision: **REVISE**

Authority: `references/00_s_type_modeling_image_v1.png`

Candidate: `output/S-authority-r0-a4-v2.blend`

## What v2 fixes

- the foot is no longer tiny;
- FRONT has a substantial split-foot base;
- FRONT34 shows a real specialized contact structure rather than tiny fork tips;
- accepted A1/A2/A3 geometry remains fixed.

## Why v2 still fails

The correction overshot in the sagittal plane.

SIDE and FRONT34 now read as a flat forward ski / duck-foot wedge. The authority FOOT STRUCTURE uses a more vertical split-hoof/blade construction: two dominant blades rise separately toward the ankle and angle down/forward, with a shorter contact length.

The single broad central base/cuff also hides too much of the split in FRONT/BACK.

## R0-A4 v3

Do not accumulate from v2. Source remains accepted `output/S-authority-r0-a3-v2.blend`.

- remove the single broad central base;
- let both dominant blades remain separate up to the ankle;
- reduce forward length from v2;
- increase vertical rise and diagonal slope;
- preserve a visible central split from FRONT/BACK;
- keep one short rear stabilizer;
- all accepted body/head/crest/limb/tail geometry remains exact.

R0-A5 remains blocked.
