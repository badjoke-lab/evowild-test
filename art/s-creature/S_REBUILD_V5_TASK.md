## Structural corrections versus v4

### Crest
Do not use the wide bilateral fan/ear envelope from v4.

Use a narrow layered stack of rear-swept laminar plates:
- multiple plates, not one spike
- modest lateral separation only
- clear backward projection in SIDE
- narrow in FRONT/BACK
- roots share skull panels / emerge from cranium
- no horns, antlers, ears or floating fins

### Thorax / waist
Do not repeat the broad shield-like FRONT silhouette.

Use:
- moderate shoulder width
- thorax depth within the locked sprint range
- ventral line that rises quickly into a narrow waist
- light pelvis, no barrel body

### Limbs
Do not use uniform rods.

Use explicit low-complexity mass changes at:
- fore shoulder / upper / elbow / distal / wrist
- hind thigh / knee / lower / hock / distal
Fore and hind chains must be visibly different.

### Posture
Lower and compact the racing stance without copying horse/deer/dog anatomy.
The body must read fast before detail or materials.
