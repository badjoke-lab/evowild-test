# S Authority R0-A5 v2 Review

Decision: **KEEP / ACCEPT R0-A5**

Actual reviewed views: SIDE / FRONT / FRONT34 / REAR34 / BACK.

## Why v2 passes

R0-A5 is a tail morphology / silhouette gate.

Compared with v1:

- the tail no longer reads as a horizontal bundle of equal paper strips;
- the continuous core now flows rearward and downward;
- blade roots and tips are staggered;
- upper / center / middle / lower layers have distinct lengths and heights;
- SIDE shows a trailing layered fan;
- FRONT34 shows separated overlapping blade hierarchy;
- BACK collapses into a coherent central trailing fan instead of a radial star;
- accepted A1-A4 geometry remains fixed.

## Qualification

Blade edge shape, thickness transitions and root integration are not final surfacing. Those remain for the post-R0 refinement phase.
