## Why v2 passes R0-A1

The R0-A1 gate is a morphology/silhouette gate, not final surface polish.

v2 now establishes the required authority-driven head/crest/neck read:

- the head is a clearly elongated forward wedge rather than the rejected blunt deer/gazelle head;
- two dominant broad crest blades own the silhouette;
- SIDE shows a long rear/upward aerodynamic sweep rather than v1's steep sail;
- FRONT and BACK preserve a clearly separated bilateral blade pair and no longer collapse into thin comb prongs;
- compact secondary blades remain subordinate at the skull base;
- the neck no longer uses v1's sinusoidal mid-neck inflation and instead reads as a controlled head-to-root taper;
- the head/neck line is lower and more forward than the rejected B2a baseline.

## Scope qualification

This acceptance does **not** claim final detail, materials, surface anatomy or production topology.

The body remains globally wrong because R0-A2 through R0-A5 have not yet been rebuilt. In particular, the old donor shoulder/chest, waist, pelvis, legs, generic feet and tail must not be used to judge the accepted R0-A1 head/crest/neck in isolation.
