A leading positive extension after its negative fold was authored; the upper-arm advance then moved earlier. The intermediate9.595cm height separation failed the new10cm review guard; the unchanged test now measures15.456cm. This is a path/timing sequence, not a higher-lift or larger-amplitude shortcut.
3. Additional .72 speed-boundary coverage exposed an inherited v2 native body corner. The v3-only short GLB tangent bridge fixed it; actual stance anchors and prior D2-D/D3-B behavior remain protected.

The fold/extension refinement preserves v2's body/rear poses throughout the requested .14–.473 window (measured zero actual-body/hind-sole drift). Thus it improves when the forelimb follows the existing rear push, but it cannot create a stronger chest/pelvis drive that is absent from that preserved sequence. No floor-force simulation is claimed. The inherited gray model's upright neck and unapproved canonical likeness remain separate limitations; no R9 or other sculpt was merged.
